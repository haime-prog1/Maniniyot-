    Cinematic tips

    • Shoot in 4K at 60fps if you can, so you can slow clips down later.
    • Keep each clip 3–5 seconds long, and move the camera slowly and steadily.
    • Golden hour (just after sunrise or before sunset) gives the best light.
    • Mix wide, medium, and close-up shots so the reel doesn't feel repetitive.
    • Cut your clips to the beat of a trending song when you edit.
